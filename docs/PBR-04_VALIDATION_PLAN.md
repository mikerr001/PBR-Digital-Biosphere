# PBR-04 Validation Plan
## Geometry
Measure internal width, height, optical path, fill volume and headspace. Record uncertainty and fabrication tolerances. Compare measured and calculated volume; do not silently reconcile mismatch.

## Light attenuation
Measure incident and depth-resolved PAR at multiple biomass concentrations, optical paths and spectral conditions. Fit effective biomass and background attenuation coefficients with confidence intervals. Validate on held-out biomass/path combinations.

## Gas transfer
Measure O₂ and CO₂ kLa independently under specified temperature, medium, salinity, gas flow, sparger and agitation. Record dissolved and headspace concentrations concurrently. For CO₂, distinguish molecular dissolved CO₂ from total DIC and measure pH/alkalinity for future carbonate speciation.

## Thermal response
Apply irradiance and ambient-temperature steps. Measure liquid and wall temperature transients. Fit absorbed fraction and UA; test whether a lumped liquid temperature is adequate.

## Mixing
Use tracer experiments to measure mixing time and spatial gradients at the intended operating conditions. Select a well-mixed, compartment or spatial model based on measured gradients.

## Integration gate
- Geometry closes within a predeclared tolerance.
- Optical model predicts held-out attenuation profiles within predeclared error.
- kLa and Henry constants have traceable provenance and stated temperature basis.
- Transfer-only liquid/gas inventory derivatives cancel exactly.
- Thermal model predicts held-out temperature transients within predeclared error.
- PBR-02 coupling passes dimensional analysis, conservation checks and independent biological validation.
