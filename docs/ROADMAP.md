# Roadmap
- PBR-01: biological foundations and evidence register; organism-specific parameter extraction remains open.
- PBR-02: reduced algal physiology and balances implemented; source-fitted parameters and independent biological validation remain open.
- PBR-03: reduced circadian phase/entrainment and acclimation foundations implemented; fitted PRCs and metabolic coupling remain open.
- PBR-04: flat-panel geometry, 1-D attenuation, two-phase gas transfer and lumped thermal model implemented; calibration and hydrodynamic validation remain open.
- PBR-02/PBR-04 integration: first dynamic coupling implemented for biomass-dependent PAR attenuation, dissolved/headspace O2 transfer and liquid temperature. CO2 gas transfer remains blocked pending carbonate speciation; independent experimental validation remains open.
- PBR-05: deterministic supervisory recommendation layer implemented with fail-closed sensor-quality handling and operator confirmation. Predictive/MPC control, sensor/actuator hardware, hardware-in-loop and safety review remain open.
- PBR-06: crop/human/resource compartments and habitat-level balances, after component-level balances are validated.
